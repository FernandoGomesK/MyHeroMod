[h1]Features[/h1]
This mod is heavily inspired by My Hero Academia and the spin-off My Hero Academia: Vigilantes. It adds items, armors, and systems that recreate the powerful Quirks of various heroes and villains from the universe directly into Terraria.

[b]All enemy mobs can spawn with a random quirk![/b]

[hr][/hr]

[h1]Added Items[/h1]

[h2]🧬 Quirk Gene (Material)[/h2]
[list]
[] Dropped by any enemy with a 50% chance.
[] Used to awaken and roll your Quirks.
[/list]

[h2]💉 Syringes & Consumables[/h2]
[list]
[] [b]Empty Syringe:[/b] Fill it with a Quirk Gene to roll and receive a random Quirk.
[*] [b]Mystery Helmet:[/b] A suspicious item used to summon the Symbol of Evil.
[/list]

[h2]🛠️ Support Items (Accessories)[/h2]
[list]
[] [b]Aizawa Goggle:[/b] Increases the time you can maintain Erasure active.
[] [b]Iron Soles:[/b] Increases the damage of kick-based ultimate moves (like Manchester Smash and St. Louis Smash).
[] [b]Air Force Gloves:[/b] Drastically increases the damage and range of Delaware Smash.
[] [b]Surge Arm Gauntlets:[/b] Increases the damage of Fire and Ice-based moves by 50%.
[] [b]Grenadier Bracers:[/b] Stores excess "Sweat" to empower Explosion attacks without hurting the user.
[] [b]Combat Vest Alpha / Beta:[/b] Increases the body's natural temperature regulation, vital for Half-Cold Half-Hot users.
[/list]

[h2]👕 Hero Costumes (Armor/Vanity)[/h2]
Tons of iconic outfits added, including Deku's Costumes (Alpha through Zeta), All Might (Golden, Silver, and Young Age), Bakugo, Todoroki, Endeavor, Dabi, U.A. Uniforms, Koichi (The Crawler), and Captain Celebrity!

[hr][/hr]

[h1]NPCs & Bosses[/h1]
[list]
[] [b]Dr. Garaki (Town NPC):[/b] Moves in when you hold a Quirk Gene. He is your main source for Quirk Syringes!
[*] [b]All For One (Boss):[/b] A devastating boss fight featuring multiple phases and iconic attacks. Survive his ultimate combinations to claim true power!
[/list]

[h1]Progression System (Quirk Stages)[/h1]
Your Quirk automatically evolves as you defeat key bosses in the world. Damage and mechanics scale to keep you balanced with Vanilla Terraria.

[list]
[] [b]Initial Stage:[/b] Default state at the start of the game. Raw and dangerous power.
[] [b]Adequation Stage:[/b] Unlocked by defeating the Eye of Cthulhu.
[] [b]Intermediate Stage:[/b] Unlocked by entering Hardmode (Defeating the Wall of Flesh).
[] [b]Advanced Stage:[/b] Unlocked by defeating Plantera.
[*] [b]Final Stage:[/b] Unlocked by defeating the Moon Lord.
[/list]
