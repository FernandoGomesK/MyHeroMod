[h1]Features[/h1]
Bring the powers of [b]My Hero Academia[/b] into Terraria! 
[b]All enemy mobs can spawn with a random quirk![/b]

[h2]Core Mechanics[/h2]
[list]
[*] [b]Multi-Quirk & Natures:[/b] Hold multiple quirks managed by a strict Quirk Limit, and roll for Natures that uniquely alter your stats.
[*] [b]Resource UI & Visuals:[/b] Dedicated interface bars (e.g., Sweat, Nausea, Charge) and comic-style onomatopoeia for iconic attacks.
[*] [b]Dynamic Progression:[/b] Quirks automatically evolve and scale in power as you defeat major vanilla bosses (from Eye of Cthulhu to Moon Lord).
[/list]

[hr]

[h1]New Content[/h1]
[list]
[*] [b]28+ Playable Quirks:[/b] Master One For All (now with Full Cowling), Explosion, Half-Cold Half-Hot, Overhaul, Zero Gravity, All For One, and many more.
[*] [b]Genetics System:[/b] Enemies have a 50% chance to drop [b]Quirk Genes[/b]. Use Syringes to roll for powers, Removers to free up slots, and Essences to target specific abilities.
[*] [b]Support Items & Vanity:[/b] Boost your abilities or remove drawbacks with accessories like Air Force Gloves and Grenadier Bracers. Dress the part with dozens of iconic Hero and Villain costumes!
[*] [b]NPCs & Bosses:[/b] Buy supplies from Dr. Garaki and Mei Hatsume (Deku NPC currently being sprited!), and survive a devastating multi-phase boss fight against [b]All For One[/b].
[/list]

[hr]

[h1]Notes[/h1]
[b]Multiplayer:[/b] Syncing is supported, but compatibility with heavy modpacks may be iffy and desyncs can occur.
